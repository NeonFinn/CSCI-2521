One Piece

Current Status: Season 21

Available Platforms: Crunchyroll

One Piece follows the adventures of a boy named Monkey D. Luffy, who wants to become king of the pirates. He can obtain this title by aquiring the One Piece. Along the way, he recruits many people to join his crew. Even though Luffy is a pirate he still has very strict morals he stands by. While sailing through the grand line to find his treasure, Monkey D. Luffy and his crew make stops at various islands. At those islands they often end up solving a problem the inhabitants are having. This leads Luffy to become a beloved figure by the people he has saved. However, he is actively hunted by the marines for his status as a pirate. Luffy continues to search for the One Piece as the show enters its final seasons.
